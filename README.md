Project title:505 Library
Description:This website is built especially for readers for all ages.It has around 6 webpages,where users can buy their next reads,gift cards,keep track of bestselling books,and view their orders' status.
Technologies used:HTML,images,CSS,JS.
Main features:The home page has a welcome message for users and a shop button that links them to the books page.
The bestsellers page has the bestselling books with their ratings.
The gif cards page has our best gift cards,in addition to our new offer where users can get 3 gift cards for 5$ only.When the user adds a gift card to their cart it asks them for the selected number of cards,from and to who this card is and then displays the price.
The order status page allows the users to review all information about the orders' time taken for delivery,etc.
The upcoming events page,allows 505 Library's users to keep track on all the upcoming events we are going to hold.
Instructions:1.To start directly shopping and looking for books click the shop now button or the word "books" in the navbar.
2.To add a book to your cart,click on the cart icon.
3.To view the bestselling books click on the work "Bestsellers" in your navbar.
4.To buy gift cards click the word "Gift Cards" found in the navbar.To add a gift card to your card,first you need to select the number of card that you want,second you need to enter what should be written on the card according to the from and to.Then,the cards will be added to ypur cart and you'll get an alert of the price.
5.To get to know about your orders' status,click on the word "Order Status" in the footer,and it will take you to the page where you will get full information about your orders' status.
6.To get to know about our upcoming events,click on the calender icon in the header or in the footer.
